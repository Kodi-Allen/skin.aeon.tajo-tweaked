# Changelog

## 1.0.0 – 2026-10-18

- Published the customization as the independent `skin.aeon.tajo.tweaked` add-on.
- Added separate Aeon Tajo Tweaked branding and versioning based on Aeon Tajo 7.7.2 for Kodi Omega.
- Fixed overlap between four-digit channel numbers and channel names in standard and compact Live TV infobars.
- Added TV show clearlogos to the Flix view while keeping episode titles visible.
- Formatted episode numbers as `SxxExx` and refined headings and runtime labels.
- Removed empty season metadata and episode statistics labels.
- Improved video information, media cases, list colours, typography, and parent-folder artwork.
- Added current Kodi 21 screenshots and English and German installation documentation.
